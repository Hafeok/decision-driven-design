## What this session is

**The last work before the repositories become public.** Four items, chosen by one test: *does this
get more expensive, or become unfixable, once someone outside can read it or fork it?*

Everything else waits. This session is not a freight sweep — the freight list keeps its remaining
items, and the transfer session that follows is governance, not canon.

**The standing reason:** after publication, a repaired defect becomes a *visible correction* against
a public record, a superseded statement may already be quoted elsewhere, and a false originality
claim on a front page is the most expensive kind of error the framework can make. All four items
below are cheap now and dear later.

## I-1 — the accountability arity *(the one that reaches canon)*

**Ruled at Paper A's Gate 3 as both-not-either:** the paper flags that canon disagrees with itself
about the relation's arity, and a canon session owes the ruling. This is that session.

`DDD-frame-08` (projected) states a five-element accountability relation. `term:accountability`
(settled) states "borne consequence" as one element where the claim refines it into stake plus
sanction path, and the claim adds **authority linkage**, which the term does not carry.

**So a projected claim adds an element to a settled term.** That is a supersession question, not an
elaboration, and it is exactly the shape that must not be published unresolved: an outside reader
finds a claim and a term that disagree, with nothing saying which governs.

**Report both texts verbatim first.** Then propose — do not assume — one of: the term supersedes to
carry five; the claim narrows to the term's three; or the two are reconciled as different objects
(the term defining the relation, the claim asserting what a *complete* instance requires) with the
distinction stated in both.

**Note the survey result that bears on it:** Paper A's related-work section found four of the five
elements are already Bovens's, and what is not his is authority linkage plus a change of tense from
retrospective assessment to design-time constitution. Whatever files must not claim more than that.

**GATE 1 — hold on both texts and the three dispositions.**
