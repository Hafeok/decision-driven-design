# Determination and Intelligence

> **Core §6 — normative.** The framework is orthogonal to intelligence, and this chapter is where it says so and why. [Determination](00-determination.md) is the primitive; intelligence is not in the primitives, not in the stores, and not required for actorhood. Keeping the two apart is what lets the framework quantify over programs, ensembles, and markets — and it is what stops the framework from making the one claim that would travel furthest and be false.

**The framework is orthogonal to intelligence, and this is a feature.**

A thermostat determines. It reads ground (the temperature), resolves a choice (call for heat or don't), and passes both [admission tests](00-determination.md#the-admission-tests-the-discipline-that-keeps-this-a-law) — vary the choice and the room's outcome moves; vary the world and the choice moves. Nobody thinks a thermostat is intelligent. **Determination is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.**

This is why the framework must **not require intelligence for actorhood.** The moment it does, it loses the program, the ensemble, the market, the immune system — every actor whose determinations are real but whose intelligence is absent or undefined — and with them the actor-general claim that makes the whole framework work. An actor is anything that determines against ground. Full stop. Intelligence is a separate question the framework declines to answer, and the decline is principled.

## The negative result

> **A closing predicate makes intelligence unnecessary.** Path-degeneracy ([Actors §2](04-actors.md#2-the-floor-is-in-the-predicate)) means adequacy is cheap wherever adequacy is checkable. Every domain that fell to machines fell because someone found a closing predicate — **not because someone built a mind.**

Chess, protein folding, competitive programming, theorem-checking: each fell when its acceptance predicate was made decidable, and then infinitely many structurally different determiners sufficed. No particular judgment was required, only an adequate one, and adequacy was cheap because it was checkable. This suggests, without proving:

> **Intelligence, whatever it is, is only load-bearing where you cannot check the work.**

Which is exactly the region where the [floor](03-the-polanyi-floor.md) is non-zero and [selection replaces training](04-actors.md#3-selection-and-training). The framework's two most useful results and its stance on intelligence are the same result seen from three sides.

## The LLM-intelligence debate is structurally undecidable

The framework declines to say whether LLMs are intelligent, and — unusually — it can say *why* the question cannot be settled by the evidence either camp brings.

**The invalid inference, refused explicitly.** *"LLMs decide on open predicates ∴ LLMs are intelligent"* affirms the consequent, and worse: it treats **unverifiability as evidence for the capacity that unverifiability makes unmeasurable.** A Magic 8-Ball also emits determinations on open predicates. *Emitting is not the criterion.* That an actor produces outputs where no check can grade them is not evidence of intelligence; it is precisely the condition under which intelligence cannot be evidenced.

**The real result:**

> **You cannot benchmark your way across an open predicate — because a benchmark *is* a closing predicate.** Construct one and you have *closed* the predicate, moving the question into the region where degeneracy says intelligence is unnecessary.
>
> **Every measurable success is in the region where intelligence is unnecessary. Every claim of intelligence is in the region that is unmeasurable.** The evidence and the claim **never occupy the same territory** — and cannot, by construction.

**It cuts both camps.** The skeptics' dismissal is as unfalsifiable as the claim they dismiss: their evidence would have to be failures on open predicates, which are equally ungradeable, so "it failed" is as unsupportable as "it succeeded." And the believers' every benchmark is *structurally evidence against its own relevance* — the better the score, the more certainly the task was reachable without the thing the score is meant to demonstrate. Each side is arguing from the territory where its evidence cannot exist.

**Falsifiable.** The result is not idle: it forbids things. Exhibit an open predicate whose performance can nonetheless be reliably assessed (then it was not open), **or** show path-degeneracy fails on some closing predicate (adequacy is *not* cheap there). Either kills the result. Neither has been done.

## The framework declines the verdict

*"Our theory proves LLMs are intelligent"* would travel further than anything else in this repository. It is also false — the theory proves the opposite, that the question is undecidable by construction — and claiming it would forfeit the framework's standing on everything else. That is the [universal-solvent failure](00-determination.md#the-admission-tests-the-discipline-that-keeps-this-a-law) the admission tests exist to prevent, arriving through the front door dressed as a headline result.

So the framework says only what it can defend: determination is real, checkable, and actor-general; intelligence is a separate question; and the region where the intelligence question lives is, by the framework's own result, the region where no benchmark can reach it. That is a smaller claim than either camp wants. It is also the only one the evidence permits.
