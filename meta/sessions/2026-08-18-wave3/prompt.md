## What this session is

Wave 3 files the framework's principle-layer claims upstream — the material Paper A will project
rather than argue. It was chartered at the Wave 2 close ("file 3.1–3.4 + H1–H5 upstream, then
Paper A as projection") and has since gained the derivation-grade material from the Q33 orbit and
the determinables addendum. Two evidence disciplines apply, and every filing declares which it is
under:

- **Derivation-grade:** the claim is derivable from ratified canon; the session's job is the
  derivation check — each premise cited by node ID, each step stated, and the check *is* the
  evidence. These can file `projected` with the derivation as basis.
- **Statement-grade:** the claim states a position canon has held in prose or Paper A material has
  asserted; it files `projected` on its falsifier's strength with its evidence debt named (the
  freight session's D-3/ground-04 pattern).

**The bar:** upstream holds 4 decisions and ~49 claims after fourteen months — constitutional
rarity is the point. Where a filing does not clear derivation or statement grade cleanly, it is
flagged and held, not softened until it passes. The addendum's routing is advisory; canon at head
governs.

## Standing note

Commit drafts before reporting at each gate, bodies marked draft-pending-ruling. The addendum and
foundation document are source material, not rulings — where canon at head disagrees with either,
canon governs and the disagreement is reported. This is the framework's central material: where a

survives scrutiny that would convince someone who wanted it to fail.
