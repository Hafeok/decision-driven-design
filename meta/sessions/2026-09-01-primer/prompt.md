## What this session is

**An operational manual for someone running the framework on a real engagement**, not an evaluation
document for someone deciding whether it is true.

It was gated on the ground migration and the migration is done. **The vocabulary is settled**: the
world-facing sense keeps the word, the held and delivered senses are named with the delivery
vocabulary, the population sense left as *deployment distribution*, and provenance is attributes
rather than an enumeration.

**The primer is a projection: generated where it can be, pinned always, regenerable.** It is never
hand-maintained beside canon. A readable twin wins its readers, drifts, and becomes an unaccountable
second canon — which is the retrieval-monopoly pathology, and the primer is the artefact most likely
to suffer it because it is deliberately more usable than the source.

Where full generation is impractical, the primer states which sections are generated and which are
hand-written-against-a-pin, and **the hand-written ones carry the pin in their own text** so
staleness is visible to a reader rather than only to an instrument.

## The primer's shape

A practitioner needs procedures, not claims. Proposed sections; the session may re-cut them and argue
why:

1. **What this is for.** One page. Governing decisions must be supplied from somewhere; the job is
   placement and escape-prevention. **Carry the declaration verbatim** — it is committed at
   `meta/the-declaration.md`, it is register-native framing rather than a claim, and its §B-3 variant
   is written for exactly this section. Do not paraphrase it; the three consumers must not drift.
2. **Filing a decision.** The applicability gate: a resolvable predicate or declared universality,
   the axis-type field, store assignment, timing. What a well-formed decision record contains.
3. **Reading an act.** What governed it, what was delivered, what the verdict was. Delivery type per
   act-site. *Filing is not encoding*, stated as a check rather than as a claim.
4. **The floor, operationally.** Does the predicate close for *this* arrangement, over material it can
   inspect? The consequence: check the work, or check the worker. **`term:floor` now defines before it
   asserts** — teach the definition, do not quote the claim at someone.
5. **Finding escape.** The generators, what each looks like in a real register, and the sweep's honest
   limit: a coverage figure is completeness relative to the declared coordinate system, so silence is
   not evidence of coverage.
6. **What this does not do.** The honest boundary, and the research material named with its status,
   so a practitioner meeting maturation or calibration elsewhere knows what it is.

**Worked examples come from real filed decisions** — the corpus test's eleven, `product-cli`'s
register, and the canon sessions' own acts. Pseudonymise anything customer-adjacent. **Never invent
one**: an invented example teaches an example, and the corpus test showed which decisions resist
expression, which is exactly what a practitioner needs to see.

**Length: report it, no band.** A primer nobody finishes is a primer nobody uses, so brevity is a
feature and subtraction is the drafting discipline.

## Two things to carry in, both earned this month

**The two-stage verification pattern** belongs in §3 and §5 wherever the primer teaches a check: a
check whose pass state is indistinguishable from its never-run state is not a check. It was learned
on a pin advance and it generalises to any instrument a practitioner will run.

**And the instruments' own limit belongs in §6**: all three checkers verify *correspondence against
the graph*, which is what makes them trustworthy and also what bounds them. The abstract of Paper A
carried a retired claim past three green checkers because prose *about* a document inherits no
citation's protection. A practitioner should know that before they trust a green run.

## Standing note

Commit drafts before reporting at each gate, bodies marked draft-pending-ruling. **The primer's
discipline is subtraction**: every sentence that is not a procedure, or the reason a procedure works,
is a deletion candidate. **A mechanic whose claim is `projected` does not enter the procedures** — it
goes in §6 with its status named. A practitioner who later discovers that half of what they were
taught was speculation will discard the half that was not.
