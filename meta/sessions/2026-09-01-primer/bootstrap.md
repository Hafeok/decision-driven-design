> meta/sessions/2026-09-01-primer/ in decision-driven-design, per DDD-dec-20.

> Fetch both repos at head — actor-indexed-determination at v5.13.0 (verify and report the tag found)
> and decision-driven-design at head.

> Rules that override anything you might infer:
>
> - Interactive drafting. Stop at every gate for Emil's ruling. Merge nothing. File nothing in canon.
> - The primer is a projection: generated where it can be, pinned always, never hand-maintained
>   beside canon. Sections that cannot be generated say so and carry the pin in their own text.
> - Gate 1 is a filter, not an audit — item 4 already did the audit work. Report claims at
>   established and reported with their kind, which expected mechanics are carried at those statuses,
>   and anything whose status looks wrong on its own evidence. Report it; never fix it.
> - The prompt's expected survivor list is a prediction to test, not a specification. Where the graph
>   disagrees, the graph governs and the disagreement is the finding.
> - §1 carries meta/the-declaration.md verbatim — its §B-3 variant is written for this section. Do
>   not paraphrase it; three consumers must not drift.
> - Worked examples come from real filed decisions — the corpus test's eleven, product-cli's
>   register, the canon sessions' own acts. Pseudonymise anything customer-adjacent. Never invent one.
> - A mechanic whose claim is projected does not enter the procedures; it goes in §6 with its status
>   named.
> - Report length without a band. Subtraction is the drafting discipline.
> - Commit drafts before reporting at each gate, bodies marked draft-pending-ruling.
>
> Two things to carry in, both learned this month: the two-stage verification pattern (a check whose
> pass state is indistinguishable from its never-run state is not a check), and the instruments' own
> limit (all three checkers verify correspondence against the graph — which is what makes them
> trustworthy and also what bounds them; Paper A's abstract carried a retired claim past three green
> checkers).
>
> Begin with step 1 and end your first report at GATE 1: the filter, the survivor set, and any status
> that looks wrong on its own evidence.
