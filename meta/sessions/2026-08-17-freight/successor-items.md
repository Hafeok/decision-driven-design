## S-1 — The actor-versus-arrangement indexing question

**Status.** Open. Named at the freight session's GATE 5, booked on Emil's ruling as **a distinct
entry, not folded into Wave 3 and not folded into Q37**.

**The question.** Should the capacity model — and with it both conjuncts of the escape mechanism —
be indexed to the **arrangement** rather than to the **actor**?

**Why it is a convergence and not a wrinkle.** Four independent sources say the same thing from four
directions, and none of them was looking for the others. The escape-reconciliation session was
convened on three-source convergence; this is four, one level down.

| # | Source | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `term:arrangement` (settled, `core/00`) | *"The unit of comparison is the arrangement, not the isolated actor."* Canon already ruled the index — for comparison. |
| 2 | `DDD-cost-05` (projected) | *"Capacity is the bits an **actor** can supply per act."* The capacity model is indexed to the actor, against (1). |
| 3 | Ground-axes note §7.3, constraint 2 | *"The same model with retrieval and tests is a different capacity from the same model without. Execution capacity per actor should read **per arrangement**, or it reintroduces the species framing removed upstream."* |
| 4 | Ground-axes note §13.7 | *"Capability is **per arrangement**, not per actor — the resolutions may live in the tooling rather than the weights."* Reached independently of §7.3, by a different construct. |

**And the fifth site, which is why this is load-bearing rather than tidy.** `term:escape-mechanism`'s
**open** conjunct is actor-indexed too — *"no verifier **the actor holds**"* — and item F-3 filed a
scope note recording that it is **ill-defined, not merely false**, where no actor was assigned. So
*both* conjuncts of the escape mechanism are indexed to an actor, and two of the four sources above
argue on independent grounds that the correct index is the arrangement. F-3's scope note and §7.3's
constraint 2 are **the same defect met from opposite ends**.

**What resolving it would touch.** `DDD-cost-05`'s statement; `term:escape-mechanism`'s
`canonical_md` (at which point F-3's scope note is promoted out of a registry comment into canonical
text, and not before — Emil, GATE 5); `DDD-floor-01`'s region; `core/11` §§2–3 and their
re-projection; possibly `term:capacity`. Supersession pattern throughout, `DDD-dec-15`'s precedent.

**What it must not become.** A re-run of `DDD-dec-15`. That decision corrected a *quantifier*
(sufficient, not necessary). This corrects an *index* (actor, or arrangement). Different defect,
same mechanism, and the second is not implied by the first.

**Travelling companion, deliberately not a merger.** Q37 (ground-indexed determination — *the actor
is a projection over the registry*) is where arrangement-indexing would get its resolution
machinery, and the two will likely be worked in the same wave. Booked separately on Emil's ruling so
it cannot dissolve into Q37's momentum unexamined: Q37 is charter-adjacent and proposes an
inversion; this item is a vocabulary reconciliation over ratified canon, and it stands or falls on
its own four sources.

**Sources to read first:** `meta/holding-note-ground-axes-rev18.md` §7.3 and §13.7; upstream
`core/graph/terms.yaml` (`term:arrangement`, `term:escape-mechanism` and its scope note);
`core/claims/DDD-cost-05.yaml` notes; `core/claims/DDD-floor-01.yaml`; `DDD-dec-15`.
