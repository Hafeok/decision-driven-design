# applications/organizations/

Organisation design as a **directory**, not a repository (`DDD-dec-05`): four claims, no
documents of its own yet. Promotion to a repository requires core-like documents and an
identified external checker — not before.

These are the organisation-design claims exactly as they stand in `core/claims/`
(`DDD-org-01..04`) — statements and falsifiers only, no new prose. All four are **projected**
and **session-authored** (flagged `UNVERIFIED — Emil review` in their claim files); they rest on
principle-layer claims pinned upstream in `graph/upstream.yaml`. Nothing here is canon.

## DDD-org-01 — graph discipline predicts organisational outcomes

*Status: projected.*

> Graph discipline — status floors at the consumer boundary, manifests, correction flow to a single canon — predicts organisational outcomes: pivot cost, trust damage from shipping projected claims as established, decision-review latency after thesis change.

**Falsifier.** Organisations measurably differing in graph discipline show no difference in pivot cost or correction latency after size and sector controls.

## DDD-org-02 — statused Stable Dependency Principle

*Status: projected.*

> The Stable Dependency Principle, statused: decisions whose reversal cost exceeds what their basis claims' hazard can absorb (high commitment level on low-status claims) concentrate pivot cost and produce incident-discovered cascades; organisations cannot see these violations because their assumption layer carries no status field.

**Falsifier.** Decisions flagged by fragility (hazard × afferent decisions × reversal cost) generate no more rework or incident findings than unflagged decisions of equal size.

## DDD-org-03 — priority as a governed decision

*Status: projected.*

> Priority as a governed decision: backlogs ordered by decisions basedOn statused value-claims — with claim priority set by value of information and decision priority by cost of delay, reversal cost, and basis readiness — churn less and predict delivered value better than intuition-ordered backlogs; roadmap churn is the cascade from silently demoted value-claims.

**Falsifier.** Teams with statused value-claims and explicit basedOn priority show no less roadmap churn or no better value prediction than matched intuition-ordered teams; or churn events do not trace to value-claim status changes on audit.

## DDD-org-04 — cross-domain retrodiction of institutional discipline

*Status: projected.*

> Cross-domain retrodiction: the intensity of an industry's institutionalised graph discipline — permits, licensure, stage-gates, mandatory inspection — is predicted by reversal cost and feedback latency. Construction and manufacturing evolved formal status floors because irreversibility forced them; software's informality is explained by cheap retries, and its formalisation pressure rises with consequence and with agent-generated code collapsing generation cost while verification cost stands.

**Falsifier.** Industries matched on reversal cost and feedback latency showing grossly different discipline intensity, or discipline intensity tracking regulation-historical accident better than the two parameters after controls.
