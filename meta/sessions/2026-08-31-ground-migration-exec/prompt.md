## The method rules the seed carries

These are not advice; they are what the predecessor session learned by getting them wrong.

1. **A classification rule whose match window can cross a clause boundary is not an anchored rule**,
   and the two classes cannot share an acceptance standard. `[^.]` does not stop at commas,
   semicolons, dashes, list items, §-refs, version numbers or decimals.
2. **Report per-rule precision, never one figure for a table.** Rule size and rule quality are
   uncorrelated: a 60-row sample across a mixed table reported 29% where the truth was 72%.
3. **Classification data cites content, never position.** Line numbers go stale; a migration executed
   by line number would have edited three wrong rows. A generated table's rows are never a wave's to
   move.
