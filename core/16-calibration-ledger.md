## 1. The instrument

<!-- ddd:embed id=term:calibration-ledger -->
> The **calibration ledger** — a claimant's record across claims whose predicates later
> closed: a matured-verdict certificate.
<!-- /ddd:embed -->

**The calibration ledger is the assurance instrument for open-predicate carriage** — a
matured-verdict certificate where no answer-keyed instrument reaches (`DDD-cost-13`, pinned).
Open-predicate carriage cannot be tested with an answer key; a claimant's record across claims
whose predicates *later closed* is evidence of exactly the thing the examination cannot
exercise: sustained carriage ahead of the verdict. It applies to human and model claimants by
the same mechanism. **Validity condition:** the instrument exists only for claimant identities
that outlive their verdict horizons; cross-identity transfer — including model version
succession — is partial at best and per-capability. *(Claim `DDD-cost-24`, projected.)*

The accrual machinery — carrier assurance accruing at verdict speed, the ledger's minimum build
time, the turnover bound's decay form — files with the time-and-assurance register (queue §11,
pending its gate).
