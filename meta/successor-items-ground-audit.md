## 1. Freight — the apparatus cites `core/00` for a definition `core/00` does not give

**Ruled at GATE 2 (Emil): record and route, do not repair. Freight-class. It survives independently
of whether the migration proceeds.**

`apparatus/encode-verify.md:21`:

> Ground is the read-only surface an actor inspects in order to act (`core/00`).

`apparatus/closure-principle.md:21`:

> The current treatment defines **ground** as *the read-only surface actors inspect in order to
> act*…

`core/00-primitives.md`, `term:ground`, **settled**:

> **Ground** — what they are determined against.

and `term:admission-test`, **settled**:

> **A fact is ground iff varying *the world* moves the outcome past tolerance.**

**"The read-only surface an actor inspects" is sense 2 — material held and inspected. "What they are
determined against", tested by varying the world, is sense 1.** Two apparatus files attribute a
sense-2 definition to a canon source that states a sense-1 one, one of them by name with a citation.

**Why this is worth more than its size.** SR-1 ruled the split real on three independent arrivals,
all of them *observations* that the word carries several objects. **This is a consequence.** The
overload has already produced a false citation across the repository seam, in a document whose whole
purpose is to state a principle correctly. It is the split's first realised cost rather than its
fourth prediction — and both the proposal and the assessment missed it, because both reasoned about
the word and neither put the two definitions side by side.

**Repairable independently of the migration**, and arguably should be: the citation is wrong today,
whatever `ground` comes to mean tomorrow.

## 3. Must-touch at the migration — `poisoned ground` changes sense without changing text

Under the GATE 3 ruling that sense 1 keeps the word, `term:poisoned-ground` becomes **a sense-3
compound of a sense-1 head word**:

> **Poisoned ground** — ground that is **present** but false: the substrate a determination **reads**
> has been corrupted…

*Present* and *reads* are the delivered object. The head word will mean the world; the compound will
mean what reached the act.

**Emil's ruling at GATE 3, recorded as the reason this cannot be left:** a term whose meaning moves
silently is worse than one whose text moves. **It is the defect `term:floor` carried, and that one
was found only because a reader tripped over it.** `term:poisoned-ground` is `settled`, so this is a
supersession and not a migration edit.

## 4. The proposal's drafting-warning instrument needs a third exception

Implementation step 7:

> Treat an unqualified use of *ground* as a drafting warning, except in quotations or historical
> notes.

**Add: and except where the word is not being used as the technical term.**

The audit counted a projected **244 unassignable occurrences**, of which the ordinary-English cluster
is the largest live component — *"new ground"*, *"common ground"*, *"the claim it grounds"*, *"its
ground has changed"*. A warning that fires on those trains every author to ignore it. **That is the
W6 scope lesson repeating**: an instrument that cannot distinguish the case it is for from the case
it is not becomes an instrument nobody reads.
