## What this session is

The migration the audit priced. **The audit is done and its rulings are made** — this session
executes them, and stops where they run out.

**The finding that makes this necessary**, and it should be stated in the session's own record: the
remedy is definitional, not a prose repair. Fifteen settled terms carry four senses of one word
between them, including the term defining what an actor is. Prose was never drifting from the
registry; it was faithfully following a definition layer that was never single-sensed.

## Standing rulings inherited — do not re-litigate

**SR-1 — S1 keeps the word.** *Ground* means conditions in the case whose variation moves the outcome
past tolerance — the world-facing sense. Ruled on canon's authority, against the counts: it is the
only sense with an admission test, three further settled terms repeat the same *against*
construction, `DDD-ground-05` makes it constitutively prior, and `term:determinable` gives it formal
structure.

**SR-2 — S3 is not a rival primitive.** Canon's S3 occurrences are verbs applied to the S1 object —
reading, held, present, delivered. One object in three conditions, not three objects. This is why the
migration is re-expression rather than re-conception, and why the delivery vocabulary fits instead of
needing a parallel invention.

**SR-3 — reuse the delivery vocabulary** (`term:delivery`, `term:undelivered`,
`term:presumed-discharge`, v5.5.0). Mint nothing beside it. `basis` remains excluded as a candidate
name for anything.

**SR-4 — provenance is independent attributes, not an enumeration.** `DDD-dec-26`'s ineligibility
ruling was on the *value*, not the axis; the enumeration's values live in three different senses, so
it was three attributes flattened.

**SR-5 — S5 leaves as *deployment distribution*.** Zero identifiers, zero pins; 29 of 74 sit in
merged papers, so it **rides with a paper revision** rather than replacing one.

**SR-6 — node identifiers do not move.** `DDD-ground-01…05` keep their names permanently. The
migration renames the **concept**, never the identifiers.

**SR-7 — counts are counted.** No estimates enter any report.

### G2 — the collision sweep

**Widened from the booked assert-not-define sweep, because it is the same registry read.**

Two sweeps, one pass over all 62 terms carrying canonical text:

1. **Assert-not-define** — canonical text that asserts a property of its subject without defining
   the subject. `term:floor` was the found instance; `term:path-degeneracy` is the next candidate.
2. **Name collision** — any term whose *name* is used in canon for a second object. **Two known
   instances, both to be confirmed and priced:**

     construction, v5.5.0). Unrelated objects; "encoded and mechanically delivered" currently reads
     as incoherent.
   - `judgment` — `term:judgment` carries an accountability clause that `DDD-frame-17`'s judgment
     *mode* cannot carry. Recorded at Phase 1a as two objects under one word.

**Report with costs; repair nothing in this gate.** Each promotion or rename is a supersession with a
pin cost, and this session's budget is the ground migration.

**GATE 2 — hold on both sweeps.**

## Ruled separately — W4, the serialised field

## Downstream demand — rule once, with these visible

Three things are queued behind whichever words this session rules. The session does not file them; it
**rules with their existence known**, so the ruling is made once.

1. **An act-ontology / verdict-ontology term pair** (Q44, Q45, and the standalone explainer).
   Candidate names for two ontologies, both of which will use whatever this session settles.
2. **`posterior` as a candidate replacement for the `mechanical` store**, if G2 confirms the
   collision. Emil's lean, not a ruling: it names the timing, parallels `encoded`, carries no
   automation implication, and collides with nothing found so far. **Two prior naming attempts died
   on registry checks** — `fixed` against `term:encoded`, `resolved` against `term:determination` —
   so treat it as a candidate to be checked, never as settled.
3. **Audience denomination.** Canon is the naming authority; projections denominate for their
   audience, and the denomination is **recorded on the term** rather than invented downstream. This
   is already canon's practice in one place — `term:verdict` carries the engineering projection's
   name as a parenthetical — and has never been named as a rule. If a `denominations:` field is
   wanted, this is the session to rule it, because the primer is the first projection that will need
   it at scale.

## Standing note

Commit drafts before reporting at each gate, bodies marked draft-pending-ruling. This migration
